# Grading system

The university grades every course on a percentage from 0 to 100. The percentage is turned into a
letter grade, and each letter is worth a number of grade points. Grade points are what the GPA is
made from.

The scale has 11 bands, from A (the highest) down to F (failing). The exact percentage ranges and
grade points of each band are in the grading scale table, which is the single source for the numbers.

A grade on the exact boundary belongs to the higher band. For example, a course result of exactly
90.0 percent is an A-, not a B+.

A course is passed with a letter worth at least the passing grade points in the table. An F means
the course was not passed and it must be repeated if it is required for the degree.

The GPA is calculated by the GPA tool only. This document never gives a GPA.

Students who disagree with a grade can ask for a review through their department. The review request
must be made within the period set by the academic calendar.
