# Course Substitution and Waiver Process

## What is a substitution?
A course substitution allows a student to fulfill a degree requirement with a different course that covers similar learning outcomes. 

## Eligibility
Substitutions are not automatic. The university maintains a list of pre-approved substitute courses for specific targets. Even if a course is on the approved list, you must have completed it with a passing grade that meets or exceeds the minimum grade requirement for that substitution. The substitute course must also meet the minimum credit requirement.

## The Waiver (Exemption) Process
A waiver is different from a substitution. A waiver asks the university to excuse a requirement entirely based on prior equivalent experience. The chatbot cannot process waivers. To request a waiver, you must speak directly with your department advisor.

## How to apply
If the chatbot indicates you are "likely eligible" for a substitution, you must still get final approval. You will need to provide your official transcript and a signed substitution form to your department advisor.
