# Exam Proctoring Policies

The university uses different proctoring modes depending on the course. 

## In-Person Proctoring
Most on-campus exams are proctored in person in designated exam halls. You must bring a government-issued photo ID that matches your student record, and your student number must be on the seating chart. 

## Online Proctoring
Courses designated as fully online use remote proctoring software (such as Proctorio). You will need a stable broadband connection, a working webcam and microphone, and you must perform a desk scan. You must show your government-issued photo ID to the camera and complete a live face check.

## Proctoring Centre
High-stakes or make-up exams may be held at a partner testing centre (such as Pearson VUE) by appointment. Your ID must match your registration exactly. 

*Note: For information about what items you can bring into the exam room (calculators, notes, etc.), please ask about Exam Rules.*
