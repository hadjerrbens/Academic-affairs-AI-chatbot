# Course registration: how it works

## Who can register
Registration is open only during the registration period published in the academic calendar.
Ask the chatbot for the current registration dates.

## Steps
1. Choose a course and a section (each section has its own instructor, schedule and seat count).
2. Check eligibility. The chatbot tells you if you can register and, if not, why.
3. Press the Register button on the course card. The chatbot never registers for you.
4. To leave a course, press Drop on the same card.

## What can block a registration (checked in this order)
1. You are already enrolled in the course.
2. Registration is closed.
3. The section is full.
4. A prerequisite is missing. A failed course (grade F) does not count.
5. The schedule overlaps with a course you are already registered for.
6. Your academic standing does not allow registration.

## Schedule conflicts
- Two classes that overlap on the same day are a hard conflict: registration is refused.
- Two classes back to back (one ends when the next starts) are fine.
- A gap shorter than 15 minutes is only a warning.
