# Transcript requests

A transcript is a record of the courses a student took and the grades received. There are two kinds.

An official transcript is signed and stamped by the registrar. It is the document that other
universities, employers and embassies accept. An unofficial transcript is a plain copy for the
student's own use and carries no stamp.

How to request one:
1. Log in to the student portal and open the transcript request page, or ask the chatbot to prepare
   a request.
2. Choose the kind of transcript and how you want to receive it: pick-up at the registrar's office,
   email (unofficial copies only), or post (official copies only).
3. Check the summary and confirm. Nothing is submitted until you confirm.
4. The registrar prepares the document. The estimated time depends on the kind and the delivery
   method and is shown when you make the request.

Who cannot request a transcript: students whose academic standing does not allow it, and students
with an unpaid hold on their account. Clear the hold or speak to your academic advisor first.

Asking for the same kind of transcript with the same delivery method twice on the same day is not
allowed. Fees and delivery times are shown by the chatbot when you make the request.
